- Edith now runs natively on the Mac: SFTP, FTP and FTPS, with Monaco as the editor.
- Import your servers from the Linux app, including passwords from an encrypted export, or from a FileZilla sitemanager.xml.
- Files keep their encoding and line endings: Latin-1 and CRLF files are saved back unchanged.
- FTPS checks certificates and resumes TLS sessions, so servers like vsftpd work out of the box.
- Open files in any Mac app with Open With; saving there uploads them again.
